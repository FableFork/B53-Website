Synopsis

Created while working at Sentient by Elysian, this project was commissioned as the main stage opening video for the Dubai AI Festival 2025.

The challenge was both ambitious and time-critical: producing a fully CG, large-scale film within just 12 days, tailored for an ultra-wide stage format of 6400 × 1300 pixels.

Conceptually, the film explores the metaphor of the "black box mind" of artificial intelligence—a space where thoughts form, collide, and evolve.

  • Fully CG production in 12 days
  • Ultra-wide 6400 × 1300 stage format
  • Abstract visual storytelling — past, present, future of AI
